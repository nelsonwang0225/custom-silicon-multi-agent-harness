# Quality Hold & Disposition SOP

SYNTHETIC controlled procedure DOC-QA-HOLD-01, version 1. Approved, effective
August 1, 2026. Business owner: Product Quality / Manufacturing. Controlled
publication: Engineering Hub. Applies to Helios Atlas Inference, PRG-A17,
ACCELERATOR-X REV-B, configuration CFG-B-01, and yield exception recovery.

## Held material and shipment eligibility

Material on quality hold is not eligible for customer allocation, shipment or
release until the required disposition process is complete. The current
Manufacturing Portal lot restrictions and material disposition are authoritative.
A document, forecast or historical report cannot remove a current HOLD.

First-pass passing units inside a held lot remain held inventory. Passing an
initial test does not establish release, customer acceptance or shipment
eligibility. Count only released, eligible material in supply calculations;
obtain quantities from the current Manufacturing Portal records.

## Investigation while held

Standard investigation activities may proceed while the lot remains held when
the approved, applicable investigation procedure permits them. Collect and review
existing test logs, verify comparability and prepare evidence for Quality review.
Routing an investigation or approving a recovery planning task does not authorize
physical retesting, rework, release or customer allocation.

## Disposition authority and traceability

The authorized Quality lead owns the final release, reject, rework or retest
disposition under the applicable policy. Record the decision and its lot, test
program and evidence references in the authoritative source before changing
material availability. Non-standard work requires the appropriate human authority.
Agents and automated systems do not independently release held material or grant
disposition authority. The current recovery workflow exposes no hold-release action.
