# PROC-LC-02 — supplemental workload evidence procedure

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT. Program PRG-A17. Effective before the scenario clock.

For target workload WF-LC-V2, use the exact approved configuration and an eligible
sample with no applicable manufacturing restriction. Run the 480-minute suite
across three input-token bins, 160 minutes per bin. Include 120 minutes for setup.
The lab slot starts with setup; workload execution begins after setup completes.
Record the model/configuration versions, observed functional behavior, power and
thermal telemetry, throughput and latency measurements, and the unchanged
acceptance-criteria evaluation. Do not weaken limits to obtain a passing result.

Check that the lab supports CFG-B-01 and the procedure, that the sample is available,
and that partner restriction data is no more than 24 scenario hours old. A prior
shorter run or a result on another product revision does not complete this obligation.
Allow 240 elapsed minutes of engineering review after the test. This mock procedure
uses a simplified 24/7 review calendar; it is not a production scheduling standard.
A successful result becomes evidence for disposition, not automatic acceptance.
