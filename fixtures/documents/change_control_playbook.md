# Customer-change coordination playbook

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT for PRG-A17.
On a customer request, identify the exact requirement delta and applicable product
revision. Separate established facts, missing evidence, and unverified assumptions.
Use authorized records from engineering, the validation lab, manufacturing partner
view, ERP, and program planning to identify permissible responses.

Compare total setup/execution/review timing and approved incremental costs.
A qualified option can still miss a milestone. An early option is not usable if
its lab, sample, or procedure is ineligible. Present alternatives and uncertainties
for the authorized decision; do not guarantee a passing future test.

After approval, execute only the plan's named actions. Preserve baseline dates and
customer commitments, link the lab job to the change and milestone, and read back
each write. If one system update fails, preserve acknowledged successes and resume
only missing work. Report scheduled, executed, reviewed, and accepted as distinct
states. Review source freshness and versions before committing consequential actions.
