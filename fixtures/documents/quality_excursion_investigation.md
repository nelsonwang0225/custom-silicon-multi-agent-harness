# Final-Test Excursion Investigation Procedure

SYNTHETIC controlled procedure DOC-QA-EXCURSION-01, version 1. Approved, effective
August 1, 2026. Business owner: Product / Test Engineering with Product Quality /
Manufacturing. Applies to PRG-A17, ACCELERATOR-X REV-B, CFG-B-01, and yield
exception recovery. This explains investigation practice; the current Engineering
procedure PROC-QE-B-01 determines the approved task scope and routing authority.

## Investigating a final-test excursion

Confirm the affected lot, product revision, configuration and test stage using
current source records. Verify the result denominator, tested quantity, first-pass
passing quantity and failing quantity before comparing pass rates. Do not mix
initial results with later repeats or interpret first-pass passes as released stock.

Confirm the test-program revision before comparing to a baseline. Check that the
baseline and observation refer to comparable populations, configuration revisions,
test stages, sites, equipment families and test conditions. Missing comparability
evidence leaves the comparison unresolved; it does not prove material degradation.

## Site and equipment investigation

Review failures by test site and equipment with Quality. Failure concentration at
one site or tester is an investigation signal, not a confirmed root cause. Consider
equipment, test-program and material explanations and collect discriminating
evidence before selecting corrective action. Scope related lots only when evidence
supports the relationship; proximity or a shared label alone is insufficient.

Preserve traceability to the lot, test-program revision, configuration, source
observation, baseline and reviewed evidence. Keep hypotheses separate from
established findings. Historical precedent supports investigation questions, not
current causality. Non-standard retest, rework or disposition requires appropriate
human authorization. Follow DOC-QA-HOLD-01 while the material remains held.
