## Prior experience with failures concentrated at one test site

A prior fictional engineering lot showed failures concentrated at one final-test
site. The team reviewed tester and equipment logs, checked the test-program
revision and setup conditions, and examined material traceability. The initial
site pattern supported several hypotheses; concentration itself did not establish
a root cause. No corrective action was justified by the distribution alone.

Additional controlled comparison evidence was required before corrective action.
After separate human authorization, the team compared setup records and repeated
measurements, then documented a site setup inconsistency in that prior case. That
finding applied only to the reviewed historical evidence. Closure of the report
did not constitute a general lot-release instruction or establish any current
lot's availability.
