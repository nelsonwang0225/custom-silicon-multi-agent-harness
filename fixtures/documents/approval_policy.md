# Supplemental validation approval policy

SYNTHETIC POLICY POLICY-APP-01 for PRG-A17.
The designated demo engineering approver may authorize PROC-LC-02 on an eligible
configuration, sample, and lab, with incremental spend up to 200,000 USD cents.
Other cases require escalation. This delegated combined authority is a demo
simplification; it is not a statement about normal industry approval roles.

Approval must identify an immutable plan, its version/digest, selected resources,
permitted actions, cost, and relevant source versions. A changed request, procedure,
restriction, or out-of-scope action requires reassessment. Automation cannot approve
itself. A caller-provided string claiming approval is insufficient.

The approved actions are lab-job scheduling/resource reservation and linking that
job into the internal program plan. They do not authorize changing ERP commitments,
releasing a manufacturing hold, altering acceptance limits, or authoring test results.
